Welcome

Every Sunday, all year round, we meet at Veyrier-Douane near Geneva to hike up the Salève. Everybody welcome. A representative of the Association Genevoise des Amis du Salève will be waiting for you in any weather. No need to sign up in advance, just come. All you need to know is found on this page.

You need to be in good shape and wear appropriate footwear (no sandals, flip-flops, etc!) The Salève is a real mountain, where you have to be careful.
Be sure to bring enough water, as there is no drinking water along the trails.

Also take a hat and sunscreen, one or two hiking poles (optional), a wind- or rainproof jacket, warm clothes in winter and snow spikes if it's icy, passport, some money, and a picnic.

We meet at 10:00 am at the terminus of bus 8 in Veyrier-Douane , 100 meters from the border on the Swiss side. Departure on foot from there. The choice of route(s) is made on the day, depending on the number of volunteer guides present and the abilities of the participants (more info here in French). Expect 4-6 hours of walking, including 2-3 hours of ascent at a steady pace with some 850 meters of altitude difference. Possibility to get down by cable car.

Additional hikes

Sometimes we organise an additional hike the same day from a different meet-up point to explore the South of Mont Salève. These hikes tend to be longer and far from the cable car, so for experienced hikers only. Details are confirmed below one or two days in advance, depending on weather conditions.
